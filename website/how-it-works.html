Hold. Talk. Done.

A few shortcuts and a few things you can say. That's all there's to learn.

  1. Hold the shortcut

    Click in any text box and hold Ctrl + Win.

  2. Talk

    Take your time. Pause to think. Let go when you're done.

  3. Read it where you typed

    Murmint formats your words once, pastes them at your cursor and restores your clipboard.

Just talk

"I need milk, eggs, bread and coffee"A bulleted list, without asking
"apple, orange, grape"A bulleted list
"first book the venue second send the invites"A numbered list
"ten percent off, five dollars"10% off, $5
A request to Claude, ChatGPT or CursorA clear, numbered prompt

There are no commands to learn. Murmint works out what you mean from how you say it.

From your voice to your cursor

One small native core

Everything except the interface runs in a single Rust process of about 4.5 MB: the rules, the prompts, the model router, your keys, the shortcuts and the typing. The interface is a thin window and a tray icon.

A style for each app

Murmint notices the app you're typing in. On the Apps page you add the ones you want to treat differently and pick a style for each, so Slack can be professional while Discord stays casual. Apps you haven't added use your default style.
