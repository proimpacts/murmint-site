Your first meeting

What happens, what to bring, and the words you'll hear. Nobody expects anything of you. You can just listen.

Before you arrive

You don't need much. Most people already have all of it.

A microphoneThe one in your laptop or headset is fine.
Windows 10 or 11That's where the meetings are held.
MurmintA free download. It takes about two minutes.
A cup of teaOptional, but strongly encouraged.

How a meeting goes

  1. WelcomeThe chair says hello and reminds everyone that phones are on silent and keyboards are face down.
  2. Introductions"Hi, I'm Sam, and I'm a typer." You can say your name, or just "Hi, I'm new." Both get a warm "Hi" back.
  3. The readingSomeone reads today's Just for Today page out loud. Slowly. Nobody types it up.
  4. SharesMembers talk about their week: the long email they said instead, the reply they didn't rewrite. Nobody interrupts, and nobody corrects anyone's grammar.
  5. KeycapsAnyone marking a milestone picks up a keycap. There's clapping. It's quieter than typing.
  6. ClosingEveryone says it together: "One sentence at a time." Then more tea.

Words you'll hear

Typer
Anyone who types more than they'd like to. Most of us, at first.
Share
A few minutes of talking about how it's going. Always spoken, never typed.
Hold
Pressing Ctrl + Win and talking. The first step, every time.
Keycap
What we hand out for milestones, from "Welcome" to "Multiple years".
Sponsor
A member with a bit more time keyboard-free who'll answer your questions. Usually by voice note.
Slip
Typing out something long when you could have said it. It happens. Nobody minds.
Cross-talk
Replying in the middle of someone's share. We don't, unless it's "same".
The Basic Shortcut
Our main text. Most of it was dictated.

A few gentle rules

What's said here stays here.

You never have to share. Listening counts.

No correcting anyone's grammar. Murmint does that.

Please leave the loud mechanical keyboards at home. We can hear them.
