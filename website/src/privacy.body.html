What leaves your PC, and what does not.

Murmint is built so you can see exactly where your words go.

Audio

Your voice goes to the speech provider you chose, only while you're dictating. Murmint never stores your audio.

Text

The words go to the formatting provider you chose to be cleaned up. That's the only other place they go. With a model on your own PC through Ollama or LM Studio, your text never leaves your computer.

API keys

Keys are encrypted with Windows and only ever sent to their own provider.

History

History is optional. It's kept only on your PC, and you can clear it at any time.

What isn't sent

  • Window titles are never sent.
  • Your vocabulary is only used to spell your words correctly, and you can turn off sending it.
  • Nothing is used to identify you or your voice. Vocabulary recordings only teach Murmint how the speech recognizer hears a word.

The code

Everything Murmint does is in its source code. You can read it, and you can build the installer yourself.
