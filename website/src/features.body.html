Everything between your voice and the text box.

Murmint does one job: it turns what you say into clean text where your cursor is. Here's how.

Listen

Ready the moment you press the shortcut.

Live transcription

Your words go to your speech provider while you talk, over one connection that stays open, so nothing waits for you to finish. The microphone stays ready for a while after each dictation, so the next one starts at once.

Listening

Three ways to dictate

Hold the shortcut and let go when you're done. Click once to start and Murmint stops by itself after a few seconds of quiet. Or go hands-free and it types after every pause.

Clean

It fixes the writing, never the meaning.

Fillers and repeats removed

"Um", "uh" and accidental repeats are dropped. It may smooth a few words so the sentence reads well, but every idea stays and long dictations are never shortened.

So, um, I wanted to to send you the notes from today's meeting.

Punctuation that follows your pauses

Murmint measures how long you paused. A pause where a new part of the sentence starts becomes a comma, a pause in the middle of a phrase adds nothing, and a long pause after a full sentence ends it.

When I got home pause I noticed the door was open.

When I got home, I noticed the door was open.

Lists, automatically

Name three or more things in a row and they become a list, with any lead-in words kept above it. Counting off or saying "numbered list" works too.

Small things

The details that save you a trip to the keyboard.

Say the punctuation

Say "question mark", "exclamation mark", "comma", "colon" or "semicolon" and you get the character. If the transcript already put one there, it is not doubled.

What time is the meeting question mark?

Emoji by voice

Say "thumbs up emoji", "heart emoji" or "fire emoji" and the emoji is typed where you are.

Nice work thumbs up emoji👍

Clean up what you already typed

Select text you wrote yourself and press Ctrl + Alt + F. Murmint formats it in place, the same way it would a dictation.

Selected
hey can you send the report by friday thanks

Fixed
Hey, can you send the report by Friday? Thanks.

Undo and cancel

Press Ctrl + Alt + Z to remove the last thing Murmint typed. Press Esc to cancel a dictation before anything is typed.
